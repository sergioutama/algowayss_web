Our services

development.

We work on both sides of reporting, with the people who set the rules and the teams who prepare the data.

Regulatory reporting & XBRL

We convert financial data into Extensible Business Reporting Language (XBRL), check it against reporting rules and help reporting teams understand validation errors.

Our early tools included an Excel add-in, XIRY (XBRL Integrated Reporting System) and Standard and Ultimate editions of our SLIK application. Multifinance companies could also send us their spreadsheets for conversion and validation.

Data governance & architecture

We review reporting applications and business processes, design metadata and taxonomies, and harmonise reference data across systems.

Software development & integration

We build web, desktop and mobile applications, Excel add-ins and middleware. We connect data sources so teams can enter a record once and reuse it.

AI integration & knowledge management

We explore how AI can help teams find answers in their own documents. Our internal knowledge base brings together a curated wiki, document search and a record of past conversations so colleagues can pick up where earlier work left off.

Implementation, training & support

We deploy cloud applications, test access controls and work with users on acceptance testing. We train operators, hand over source code and recovery guides, and provide remote support.

Selected work

Reporting in practice.

Otoritas Jasa Keuangan

Designing for integrated financial reporting

On World Bank-funded assignments for Indonesia’s Financial Services Authority since 2018, we reviewed the applications and business processes behind regulatory reporting.

We designed taxonomies for banking, insurance and capital markets, harmonised reference data for OJK’s warehouses and advised its staff on an integrated reporting architecture.

Reporting platforms

From Excel to validated XBRL

For multifinance companies, we converted spreadsheets into validated files for submission to OJK. For listed companies, we built a platform that checks Excel uploads against the IDX Taxonomy 2020 and returns a validation report.

Industry training

XBRL for reporting teams

We led XBRL training for regional development banks through ASBANDA and for PT Bank Mizuho Indonesia. We also introduced Sharia banking teams to Bank Indonesia’s reporting platform.

Product development

Tools for day-to-day decisions

For HM Sampoerna, we built an Excel add-in for sales route planning and an application for tracking marketing activity. Our other products include a resident portal and a public health assessment tool.

Our consultants work from Indonesia and abroad. Across the team, we bring experience in software engineering, data analysis and financial reporting.

How we work

  1. Understand the process

    We sit down with the people doing the work and trace how information moves through their systems.

  2. Agree on the design

    We define requirements together and prototype the parts users need to try first.

  3. Build and test in stages

    We test components and integrations, then review each increment with its users.

  4. Hand over and support

    Operators receive training, source code and guides for deployment, rollback and recovery.
