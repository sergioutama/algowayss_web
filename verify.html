Verification steps

How to verify a signed document

  1. Download the public key and confirm its fingerprint matches this page (6FD3 4635 EBC0 E8A6 5B64 F66F EBB3 61AE ED23 F9AF):

    gpg --show-keys algoway-signing-key.asc

  2. Import the key into your keyring:

    gpg --import algoway-signing-key.asc

  3. Verify the detached signature alongside the document:

    gpg --verify <document>.pdf.asc <document>.pdf

    The output must report Good signature from "PT Algoway System Solution <meutia@algowayss.co>" with primary key fingerprint 6FD3 4635 EBC0 E8A6 5B64 F66F EBB3 61AE ED23 F9AF.

On Windows, use Gpg4win (Kleopatra): import algoway-signing-key.asc, then choose “Decrypt/Verify” on the .asc signature file.

Additional checks

Other checks

  • A SHA-256 checksum of each file, listed in the cover email.
  • An OpenTimestamps .ots proof, verifiable at opentimestamps.org.
  • For Indonesian documents, an e-meterai, verifiable at verification.peruri.co.id.

If something doesn’t match

Do not rely on the document. Contact us directly through the site’s contact form so we can confirm whether the file is genuine.
