### Key Metrics to Monitor

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Page views (unique and total)
- Time on page
- Bounce rate
- Scroll depth
- Comments and feedback

**Social Metrics:**
- LinkedIn likes, comments, shares
- Twitter/X likes, retweets, replies
- Click-through rate from social posts

**Business Metrics:**
- Newsletter click-through (if included)
- Lead generation or contact form submissions
- Inbound inquiries referencing blog posts
- Website traffic from blog to other pages

### Monthly Review

At end of each month:
1. Compile performance data for all published posts
2. Identify top-performing content
3. Analyze patterns in successful posts
4. Adjust content strategy based on insights
5. Update future topics prioritization

## Flexibility and Adjustments

### Rescheduling Protocol

**If deadline missed:**
- Publish within 1 week of original date
- Update calendar with new date
- Notify team of schedule change
- Adjust subsequent posts if needed

**If urgent topic emerges:**
- Insert into next available slot
- Bump lower-priority post by one week
- Maintain overall content balance

**If major company announcement:**
- Publish special announcement post (additional to schedule)
- Adjust subsequent calendar dates accordingly
- Ensure announcement gets priority promotion

### Quality Over Quantity

**If draft not meeting quality standards:**
- Delay publication until quality threshold met
- Skip to next topic if deadline pressure
- Maintain quality bar at 80%+ on checklist

## Calendar Maintenance

### Weekly Tasks

**Every Monday:**
- Verify next Wednesday post is drafted
- Confirm content passes quality checklist
- Prepare social media assets and copy
- Schedule social media posts

**Every Wednesday (Publication Day):**
- Publish post to production
- Execute promotion schedule
- Monitor initial engagement
- Respond to early comments or feedback

**Every Friday:**
- Review performance of Wednesday's post
- Check for any issues or corrections needed
- Update performance tracking spreadsheet
- Plan next week's post work

### Monthly Tasks

**Last Friday of each month:**
- Compile monthly performance report
- Review topic balance (case study vs dev-log, impact levels)
- Identify patterns and insights
- Adjust next month's calendar based on performance
- Update future project templates with lessons learned

### Quarterly Tasks

**End of each quarter:**
- Comprehensive review of all content published
- Evaluate content strategy effectiveness
- Update prioritized topics list
- Adjust editorial calendar if needed
- Plan next quarter's content themes
